About Us

Quality Club

The Quality Club is a student initiative at BIT Sindri, started with the support of the Indian Foundation for Quality Management (IFQM). Its purpose is to teach students standard documentation, procedures, audits, process discipline, and quality checks to bridge the gap between college and industry.

Vision

To create a strong culture of quality, discipline, innovation, and excellence among students so they can meet global standards.

Mission

  • Teach quality concepts in a structured and practical way.
  • Connect classroom learning with real industry practices.
  • Promote safety, consistency, and continuous improvement.

IFQM (Indian Foundation for Quality Management)

Founded in 2023, IFQM is a Section 8, non-profit, industry-led movement with a nation-first approach to catalyze Indian industries to become globally competitive and export-capable through quality, innovation, and excellence.

Role of IFQM:

Guidance from Quality Experts
Exposure to Global Standards

Quality Concepts & Tools

Quality = Consistency. Doing the right thing, the right way, every time.

Doing tasks the correct way every time using step-by-step guides (Standard Operating Procedures) to eliminate mistakes and build process discipline.

Executing tasks accurately the first time to avoid the hidden Cost of Poor Quality (COPQ), which includes lost time, money, and reputation.

Fishbone Diagram: Maps causes under Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement, and Environment.
Pareto Principle: Focuses on the top 20% of causes that create 80% of problems.

Asking "Why?" systematically to locate core system errors rather than treating symptoms or blaming individuals.

Lean: Eliminates non-value-adding waste.
DMAIC: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control roadmap for solving complex problems.

Systematic health checks and workplace organization practices designed to maintain safety and ongoing standards.
